Spec:     §7.1 state machine, §7.2 stop conditions, §7.3 feedback, §6.7 the two-phase round.

Every transition emits a `state` event. `REVISING` additionally emits one
`revise-turn` per turn (§7.1: the longest stage emitted nothing in v1). A `round`
event carries the whole snapshot, and fires TWICE for a revised round — once at
phase one (revise=null) and once at phase two, after the revise transition has
filled `revise` and `timings.reviseMs`.

`state.round` is 0 before round 1 exists — PipelineEvent.round is nonnegative()
deliberately, and consumers may not assume >= 1.
