Model residency — does sequential loading do what it claims?
============================================================
Date:   2026-07-31
Why:    A cobuilder on Linux with LM Studio wants a different model per role and
        cannot hold both resident. draft/revise use models.generator and
        critique uses models.critic, so a 3-round run with two different models
        alternates five times.
Models: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M (6.1 GB on disk) as generator
        qwen3:8b-q4_K_M            (5.2 GB on disk) as critic
        The 19.6 GB 30B was never loaded.
Host:   34.4 GB total, macOS, app and editor running.

WHAT `auto` DECIDED HERE

  AUTO -> concurrent
  reason: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M 6.1 GB + qwen3:8b-q4_K_M 5.2 GB = 11.4 GB,
          which fits within the 20.6 GB budget (60% of this machine's 34.4 GB,
          sizes from Ollama /api/tags)

  The arithmetic is real and comes from /api/tags. On a 16 GB host the same pair
  would resolve sequential, which is the case this feature exists for.

WHAT THE TWO POLICIES ACTUALLY DID

  SEQUENTIAL  (start: none resident)
    draft (generator)      resident: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M 10.1GB
    critique (critic)      resident: qwen3:8b-q4_K_M              9.8GB
    revise (generator)     resident: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M 10.2GB

  CONCURRENT  (start: none resident)
    draft (generator)      resident: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M 10.1GB
    critique (critic)      resident: qwen3:8b-q4_K_M              9.8GB
    revise (generator)     resident: qwen3-vl:8b-instruct-q4_K_M 10.1GB

THE HONEST READING: THE CONTRAST DID NOT REPRODUCE ON THIS MACHINE.

Sequential does what it claims - one model resident at a time, and the explicit
eviction is issued at each stage boundary. But CONCURRENT produced the same
trace, so this run does not demonstrate that the policy changed anything.

The cause is visible in the numbers. Each model occupies ~10 GB resident once
its 32k context is allocated, not the 5-6 GB it takes on disk, so the pair needs
~20 GB on a 34.4 GB host that was already running the app, an editor and a
browser. Ollama evicted the previous model on its own. The `auto` heuristic
compares DISK sizes (11.4 GB) because that is what /api/tags reports, and so it
concluded the pair fits - while the resident cost is nearly double that and it
did not.

TWO CONSEQUENCES, BOTH REAL

1. THE HEURISTIC UNDERSTATES RESIDENT COST, ROUGHLY 2x AT 32k CONTEXT.
   /api/tags reports on-disk bytes. Resident bytes include the KV cache, which
   scales with context length and is not reported anywhere this app can read.
   The 60% headroom factor absorbs some of this by accident rather than by
   design. A host that is genuinely marginal may be told "concurrent" and then
   thrash - which is the failure this feature was built to prevent. Anyone
   revisiting this should either measure resident size from /api/ps after a
   load, or shrink the headroom factor and say why.

2. WHAT SEQUENTIAL ACTUALLY BUYS IS DETERMINISM, NOT EVICTION.
   Ollama will evict under pressure regardless. It decides when, based on its
   own heuristics, and on a host where the pair nearly fits it may keep both and
   swap. Sequential makes the eviction happen at a known point, immediately, and
   on every host - including LM Studio, where the app's own JIT auto-evict
   setting may or may not be enabled. That is a smaller claim than "it prevents
   OOM" and it is the one the evidence supports.

WHAT IS PROVEN
  - keep_alive: 0 evicts (verified separately: a resident 10 GB model left
    `ollama ps` within 2s).
  - The runner evicts the model being LEFT, before the call that needs the
    memory, only on an actual change, and only under sequential - 1689 unit
    tests including a full-trace assertion on stage order.
  - resolveResidency reads real sizes from /api/tags and shows its arithmetic.
  - Rule zero: when both roles hold the same model - the shipped default - the
    policy short-circuits and issues nothing at all.

WHAT IS NOT PROVEN
  - That sequential prevents a failure concurrent would have suffered. Not
    reproducible on a 34.4 GB host; needs a machine where the pair genuinely
    does not fit, which is the cobuilder's.
  - Anything on LM Studio. /v1/models reports no sizes at all, so `auto` there
    falls back to an assumed 6 GB per model and says so in its reason string.
  - Anything on Linux.
