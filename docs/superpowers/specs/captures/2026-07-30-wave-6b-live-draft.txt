3. WHAT THIS MEASURES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------
Measured:
  - The gauge loop exits on the FIRST batch that clears (fox: 1 call).
  - It keeps going when the bar is not cleared, and the extra batches are
    corrective rather than decorative (tree: 2 -> 4 colours, 5 -> 11 rows).
  - A JSON Schema as `format` is accepted by Ollama 0.32.1 and constrains op
    shape: `anyOf` over five `const`-tagged variants, `additionalProperties:
    false`, and an `index` enum built from the palette in use. Every op in both
    runs parsed; nothing was dropped.
  - `meta.repairs` is 0 on both. §6.3's length repairs are unreachable here.
  - Total wall clock 16.1 s and 13.9 s, against a 45 s per-call floor. The
    floor is not the binding constraint at 16x16 — it exists so a COLD load
    cannot consume a whole call, which is what killed Wave 10's 16x16 runs.

Not measured:
  - 32x32 and 64x64. The prompt scales its coordinates and mirror axis with
    the canvas, and `draftTimeoutMs` scales past the floor at both, but no live
    run has confirmed the model scales with it.
  - Whether the sprites survive critique. That is Wave 7's question; this
    capture only shows the generator now produces something worth critiquing,
    which is what the generator benchmark said it did not.
  - Variance. One run per subject. The three-subject, repeated-run comparison
    belongs to Wave 13's bench.
