WHAT THIS SAYS
--------------
1. Shape operations are the WORST option - six times more damaging than the
   current implementation, and the only condition that degraded on every
   trial. The A10 result does NOT transfer from drafting to revision.

2. Showing the model the canvas between turns also made things worse.

3. Coverage rises in every condition while symmetry falls. The reviser adds
   pixels and breaks the silhouette, and the more expressive its tools, the
   more it adds and the more it breaks.

4. EVERY condition has a negative mean. The revise stage is net-negative
   under every configuration tested. This is not a tooling problem and
   therefore has no tooling fix.


WHY THE DRAFT FIX DOES NOT TRANSFER
------------------------------------
Drafting is composition from nothing: shape ops let the model express
"ellipse here, mirror it", which is what it is good at, and there is no
prior work to damage.

Revision is correction of something that already works. Shape ops are
powerful enough to overwrite the parts that were right - and the measured
coverage increase shows that is exactly what happens. The very
expressiveness that made drafting succeed makes revision destructive.

The critique compounds it. suggestConfidence was 0.10-0.20, so section 6.4
correctly BLANKS the suggestion - the reviser is told "your body shape is
fundamentally wrong" with no guidance on the fix. Structural feedback plus
no direction plus expressive tools is a recipe for redrawing.


DECISION
--------
Do not rebuild REVISE_TOOLS. Do not add canvas refresh. Both were measured
worse than the status quo, and the earlier single-trial result that pointed
the same way was correct for the wrong reasons (n=1 at the time).

Build the regression guard instead: compute lint() before and after each
revise pass and discard a revision that makes the sprite measurably worse.
It is the only intervention that can help, it needs no model cooperation,
and it makes the loop monotonic - it can no longer hand back something worse
than it started with. On the session the user watched, it would have kept
round 1.

The guard makes revise SAFE. Nothing measured here makes it USEFUL. Whether
a stage that is net-negative should run at all, once made harmless, is a
product question for section 11's human bar rather than a technical one.
