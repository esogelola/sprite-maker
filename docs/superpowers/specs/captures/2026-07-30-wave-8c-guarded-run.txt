   left side of the dog. The disagreement is real, and which one to believe is
   section 11's human bar, not a technical question. Note also that the guard
   ALLOWED 2/5 -> 1/5 on the previous round: the bar is structural and tracks
   the critic's rating not at all.

5. A COST WORTH NAMING. Section 6.7 says any round may be accepted. Under the
   guard the user never sees the rejected round, so on seed 22 they cannot
   choose it. The guard trades that option away for monotonicity.

6. THE ORPHAN CHECK NEVER FIRED. Across six guarded transitions, symmetry
   fired twice and coverage once. Section 6.5 defines an orphan as a cell whose
   four orthogonal neighbours are all transparent, so two adjacent detached
   cells rescue each other -- wave-11's floating yellow bar scored orphanCount
   0. The check catches detached PIXELS, not detached COMPONENTS. Keeping it
   costs nothing; it is not the check doing the work.

VERDICT
-------
The guard makes the loop monotonic on the structural metrics and it demonstrably
stops the disintegration the user watched. It does not make revise useful --
nothing measured here does -- and seed 22 shows it can refuse a round the critic
preferred. It stops the product getting worse; it does not make it better.
